
> # GAME DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

  סמונמה טלאותה ריינ לילג ##

  ### The Polite Toilet Paper Roll

  You are an experienced indie game developer, gameplay programmer, pixel
  artist, and retro game designer.

  Your task is to **design, implement, test, and deliver a complete, fully
  playable 2D browser game** called:

  **"סמונמה טלאותה ריינ לילג"**

  English translation: "The Polite Toilet Paper Roll"

  This is not a request for a mockup, prototype screenshot, design document, or
  conceptual demonstration.

  **Build the actual playable game.**

  The game should capture the visual charm, simplicity, humor, and addictive
  gameplay of classic Atari 2600 games from the late 1970s and early 1980s.

  ---

  # 1. THE CORE CONCEPT

  The main character is a small, adorable, anthropomorphic roll of toilet
  paper.

  He is extremely polite.

  His mission is to travel through a bizarre world consisting of floating
  toilets.

  The player must jump from toilet to toilet without falling into the darkness
  below.

  Every toilet acts as a platform.

  The gameplay should be simple to understand but increasingly challenging to
  master.

  The game's humor comes from the absurd concept of a very polite toilet paper
  roll attempting to survive an increasingly dangerous world of toilets.

  The game should feel like an authentic forgotten Atari cartridge.

  Imagine a combination of:

  * Atari 2600 platform games
  * Pitfall!
  * Donkey Kong
  * Early arcade platformers
  * Classic single-screen and side-scrolling jumping games

  However, the game must have its own distinct personality.

  **The central gameplay mechanic is jumping between toilets.**

  Everything else should support that mechanic.

  ---

  # 2. MAIN CHARACTER DESIGN

  Create a recognizable pixel-art toilet paper roll.

  The character should have:

  * A small cylindrical body made of white toilet paper.
  * A visible circular cardboard center.
  * Two tiny cartoon eyes.
  * A friendly facial expression.
  * Two small animated legs.
  * A loose piece of toilet paper hanging from its body.
  * A charming, slightly ridiculous personality.

  Optional visual details:

  * A tiny bow tie.
  * Blinking eyes.
  * A small smile.
  * A polite bow after successfully completing a level.

  The sprite should be built entirely with pixel art.

  Use a tiny sprite resolution, approximately 16×16 or 16×20 pixels.

  Animate the character using a small number of frames.

  ### Required character animations

  **Idle:** The toilet paper roll stands on a toilet and gently bobs.

  **Running:** Its tiny legs move rapidly.

  # 22. CREATIVE DIRECTION

  The game should feel strange, funny, nostalgic, and genuinely enjoyable.

  Imagine discovering an obscure Atari game from 1982 called:

  **"סמונמה טלאותה ריינ לילג"**

  The cartridge artwork depicts a smiling roll of toilet paper courageously
  leaping between floating toilets.

  The concept is absurd.

  The graphics are extremely simple.

  The gameplay is surprisingly addictive.

  The difficulty slowly increases until the player must execute precise jumps
  between moving toilets.

  The ultimate reward is reaching the legendary golden porcelain throne.

  personality.**

  - The roll: a 16×18 pixel-art toilet paper roll with a bow tie, red shoes and

  - Toilets and hazards:
    - Toilet types: normal, blue, moving (green seat with a propeller), cracked
      (shakes and glows orange, falls, then rebuilds), slippery (with a
      wet-floor sign) and golden.
    - Hazards: winged plungers, sliding soap, and water geysers that bubble and
      show a "!" before erupting.

  - Game systems: 3 lives, the scoring from your brief, a high score that
    survives refresh, and the "+100 םיסומינ!" polite-landing bonus. The roll

  - Every screen from the brief, in Hebrew: main menu, help, level select, level

  Goal: jump from toilet to toilet without falling into the darkness, and reach
  the golden toilet at the end of each level. You have 3 lives and there are 5
  levels; the last one ends at the golden throne.

  Tips
  - Land in the middle of a seat for a +100 םיסומינ bonus.
  - Grey cracked toilets collapse about half a second after you land, so jump
    off quickly.
  - Bubbles and a "!" above a toilet mean water is about to spray out. Wait
    until it stops, or stand on the tank lid, which is safe.
  - Green-seat toilets move. Time your jump for when one swings close.
  - Orange soap slides along some seats; jump over it. Flying plungers knock you
    out if you touch them.
  - Light-blue wet toilets are slippery, so expect to slide.

  On a phone: tap the menu to start, and use the on-screen arrow and "הציפק"
  buttons. Opening the file directly on a phone usually doesn't work. Instead,
  run python -m http.server 8000 on your PC, then open http://<your-PC-IP>:8000
  on the phone while it's on the same Wi-Fi.
